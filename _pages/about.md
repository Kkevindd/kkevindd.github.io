👋 Hi! I am Kuixuan Jiao (焦奎璇), a second-year M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU), advised by Prof. Dingwen Zhang.

🔬 My research interests include 3D/4D Scene Understanding, Spatial Reasoning, and World Models. I am particularly interested in spatially consistent scene representations and their applications to spatial intelligence and embodied AI.
